## Scope
Operator app (`/app/[scenario]/*`, Operate mode) plus the public site (`/`, Persuade) and the citizen view (`/my-area`, Read). Audience: city emergency operations duty officers and partner agencies; judges on a projector.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The cascade is a course, not a heat map. The page owns one idea: the predicted chain of failures drawn as a numbered orienteering course in a single reserved ink over a survey-map city, with a matching control description sheet. It refuses the category default of dark neon KPI cards around a glowing map.

OWN-WORLD: Survey paper ground (cool off-white, never cream), ink-black type and hairline rules, brown hex contours from real elevation, river blue, roads in graded ink. Course overprint violet is reserved for the predicted chain only. Risk states own the only other strong colour: ochre WATCH, orange HIGH RISK, crimson CRITICAL, always paired with label and shape. Line style encodes provenance: solid observed, dashed forecast, dotted inferred. Archivo across widths; tabular figures.

STORY: The officer sees what fails next, where, when and how sure the system is; follows each leg to its evidence; tests a fix and sees its side effects and deadline; decides.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed atlas map (about two-thirds width) with the course overprinted; right column is the control description sheet (numbered rows: symbol, what fails, where, P10–P90 clock window, probability). Top strip: city state, Cascade-R, issued time. Bottom: time cursor with hourly city risk bars and the NOW handle. Primary action: "Break this chain" opens the Lab on the selected leg.

FORM: Orienteering map and control-description notation (challenger 3 of the dealt hand, fused into the pinned Civic Atlas world). Seed key ebf526a1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
